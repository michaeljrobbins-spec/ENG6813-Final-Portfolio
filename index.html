Michael Robbins

“Embracing uncertainty, and giving students the freedom to explore their own process with me as their guide, is the throughline of everything I do in the classroom.”

I teach college composition with a process-oriented approach: less about handing students fixed content to master, and more about teaching them to explore a problem, evaluate their own thinking, and find direction without getting lost along the way. This portfolio brings together the materials from my ENG6813 coursework — a course syllabus, my teaching statement, and a signature assignment built for a first-year composition classroom.
